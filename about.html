Who we are

We have one goal

Clean numbers, clear answers, and a team that actually picks up the phone.

We are Ben and Rachel, the founders of Rancho Bookkeeping.

Ben has a background in Finance and Accounting, with a Bachelor's Degree in Finance and 5 years of experience in finance and accounting for local governments. He has a strong understanding of tax, helping ensure your books stay clean when they're handed off at the end of the year.

Rachel has a background in Administration and Analysis work across multiple positions she's held in the private sector.

We started Rancho Bookkeeping, located in Rancho Cucamonga, because too many small business owners were stuck with messy books, confusing reports, and wasted time that could be going towards their business.

Our goal is simple: give you clean numbers, clear answers, and more time to run your business. We reconcile everything, categorize it correctly, and answer your questions when you actually ask them, not three weeks later.

We're a two-person team, so you're never passed around. The person who does your books is the same person who answers your email.
